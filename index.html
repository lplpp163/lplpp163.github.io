About關於

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at National Taiwan University (NTU). I received my M.S. from the Graduate Institute of A.I. Cross-disciplinary Technology at National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (NTUST), and spent an exchange year at Zhejiang University. My work is in computer vision and deep learning, covering depth estimation for UAVs, perception for self-driving cars, and medical image tracking. I also teach short courses on applied AI tools.

國立臺灣大學資訊工程學系博士班在學，碩士畢業於臺灣科技大學人工智慧跨域科技研究所，並曾赴浙江大學交換一年。研究領域為電腦視覺與深度學習，做過無人機深度估測、自駕車感知與醫療影像追蹤。近期也開設 AI 工具應用的短期課程。

Project Experience專案經歷

Depth Estimation for UAVs 無人機深度估測 2023.05 – 2024.01
Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI) 工業技術研究院

Panoramic depth estimation for UAV obstacle avoidance. Handled synchronization and calibration between the camera and the IMU, tuned the feature-point algorithm for more stable positioning, and got depth estimation running on low-power onboard devices.

全景影像深度估測，用於無人機避障。處理相機與 IMU 之間的同步與校正問題，調整特徵點演算法以穩定定位，並讓深度估測能在低功耗的機上裝置執行。

Deep Learning Depth-from-Focus 深度學習 Depth-from-Focus 2022.10 – 2024.06
Kulicke & Soffa Kulicke & Soffa

Trained a depth-from-focus model that takes a stack of images captured at different focal distances and outputs a depth map together with an all-in-focus image. The architecture work from this project became a manuscript submitted to IEEE MultiMedia.

以不同對焦距離拍攝的影像堆疊為輸入，訓練深度學習模型輸出深度圖與全對焦影像。此專案的模型架構後續整理成論文，投稿 IEEE MultiMedia。

Self-Driving Car Perception 自駕車感知系統 2022.07 – 2023.03
Micromax International Corp. Micromax International Corp.

Developed self-driving functions on ROS, with adaptive cruise control as the main focus. Integrated millimeter-wave radar with deep-learning object detection to keep distance during vehicle following.

在 ROS 平台上開發自駕車功能，主要負責主動車距控制（ACC）。整合毫米波雷達與深度學習物件偵測，用於跟車時的車距判斷與控制。

Real-Time 3D Pose Estimation 即時 3D 人體姿態估測 2020.02 – 2021.01
National Taiwan University of Science and Technology 國立臺灣科技大學

Undergraduate project. Lightweight deep models paired with a ToF camera for real-time 3D pose estimation, with the coordinate stream synchronized into Unity to drive a character skeleton.

大學專題。使用輕量化深度模型搭配 ToF 相機做即時 3D 姿態估測，並將座標資料同步至 Unity，驅動角色骨架顯示。

About關於

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at National Taiwan University (NTU), advised by Prof. Chu-Song Chen. My current research is long-form video generation that keeps places and characters consistent across scene cuts. I received my M.S. from National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (NTUST), where I worked on depth from focus, and spent an exchange year at Zhejiang University. Before the Ph.D. I worked on depth estimation and localization for drones, adaptive cruise control for a self-driving car, and medical motion tracking, and I have built and run the EZQuiz exam-practice app since 2020.

Research研究

Consistent Long-Form Video Generation 跨場景一致的長篇影片生成 2025 –
National Taiwan University 國立臺灣大學

Ph.D. research. Current video models make convincing short clips but lose track of a story across scene cuts: a location changes while it is off screen, a character returns holding different things. I am building a 3D scene representation that records places, objects and character states, which later shots can query and update, and using it to condition video generation.

博士班研究。現有的影片生成模型能做出擬真的短片，但故事一換場景就容易接不上：某個地點在畫面外發生了變化，或角色帶著不同的東西回到畫面。我在做一套記錄地點、物件與角色狀態的三維場景表示，讓後面的鏡頭可以查詢、更新它，再用它來控制影片生成。

Deep Depth from Focus with Transformer 以 Transformer 做 Depth from Focus 深度估測 2022.10 – 2024.06
Kulicke & Soffa, industry–academia project Kulicke & Soffa 產學合作

Recovering the 3D structure of circuit boards from multi-focus factory captures to locate glue-dispensing points. The 1.2M-parameter Transformer model cuts depth error by 48% against the best published method on the real-world DDFF 12-Scene benchmark, and mixed-precision inference brings it from 188 ms to 10 ms. The paper is in final revision at IEEE MultiMedia. Code

在工廠用不同對焦距離拍攝電路板，從這組影像推算出板子的立體結構，找出點膠位置。模型以 Transformer 為核心，約 120 萬個參數；在真實拍攝的 DDFF 12-Scene 資料集上，深度誤差比先前最好的方法低 48%，改用混合精度推論後，單張推論時間從 188 毫秒降到 10 毫秒。論文已進入 IEEE MultiMedia 最終修訂階段。程式碼

Larynx Motion Tracking for Radiotherapy 喉癌放射治療的喉部動作追蹤 2022 – 2023
with MacKay Memorial Hospital 與馬偕紀念醫院合作

Wrote the deep-learning tracker that follows the larynx in depth-camera video while patients swallow. Its motion measurements were used in a prospective study of 7 patients to help set radiotherapy margins (Bioengineering, 2023).

負責程式：用深度學習在深度相機影片中追蹤病人吞嚥時的喉部位置。量到的移動距離用在一項 7 位病人的前瞻性研究，協助醫師設定放射治療的照射範圍，成果發表於 Bioengineering（2023）。

Projects專案經歷

Drone Obstacle Avoidance 無人機避障 2023.05 – 2024.01
ITRI, with NTU, NTHU and NTUST 工業技術研究院，與臺大、清大、臺科大合作

Moved MiDaS depth estimation from a Python prototype to C++ on an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano through ONNX, so the drone could estimate obstacle distance on board. Tuned the VINS-Fusion localization system, including camera–IMU time synchronization and calibration. Also evaluated panoramic image stitching, which was too slow for the onboard computer.

把單鏡頭深度估測模型 MiDaS 從 Python 原型轉成 C++，經 ONNX 部署到 NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano，讓無人機能在機上估計障礙物距離。調整開源定位系統 VINS-Fusion，包含相機與 IMU 的時間同步與校正。也評估過全景影像拼接，但速度太慢，無法在機上即時運作。

Adaptive Cruise Control 自駕車主動車距控制 2022.07 – 2023.03
Micromax International Corp. Micromax International Corp.

Built adaptive cruise control on ROS in C++ and Python. Combined millimeter-wave radar with a YOLO camera detector accelerated by TensorRT, filtered the sparse and noisy radar points to pick out and track the car ahead, and tested on public roads.

在 ROS 上用 C++ 與 Python 開發主動車距控制（ACC）。結合毫米波雷達與以 TensorRT 加速的 YOLO 物件偵測；雷達點少、雜訊多，我負責濾除雜訊、找出前車並持續追蹤，並實際上路測試。

EZQuiz — Exam Practice App EZQuiz 統測練習 App 2020.09 –
Founder & sole developer 創辦人／獨立開發者

A question-bank app for vocational high school students preparing for the national entrance exam, with over 5,000 downloads; monthly active users rise from about 400 to 1,800 before each April exam. Rewrote the original Kotlin app in Flutter so one codebase serves Android and iOS, with an encrypted question bank on Cloudflare and a leaderboard on Supabase. Google Play

給準備統測的高職學生使用的題庫練習 App，下載超過 5,000 次；每月活躍使用者平常約 400 人，四月考前會升到約 1,800 人。原本以 Kotlin 開發，後來用 Flutter 重寫，一份程式同時支援 Android 與 iOS；題庫加密後放在 Cloudflare，排行榜使用 Supabase。Google Play

AIoT Remote Healthcare: Real-Time 3D Pose AIoT 遠距醫療：即時 3D 姿態估測 2020.02 – 2021.01
Undergraduate capstone, NTUST 臺科大大學專題

Ran pose estimation at 14 FPS on an NVIDIA Jetson Nano (OpenPose ran at 5 FPS), combined the 2D keypoints with ToF depth to get 3D joints, and sent only the keypoints to a Unity avatar over UDP, cutting traffic from about 100 KB/s of video to 6 KB/s.

在 NVIDIA Jetson Nano 上以每秒 14 幀做人體姿態估測（OpenPose 只有每秒 5 幀），把 2D 關節點和 ToF 深度相機的資料結合成 3D 座標，再用 UDP 只傳關節點給 Unity 角色，傳輸量從傳影像的每秒約 100 KB 降到 6 KB。
